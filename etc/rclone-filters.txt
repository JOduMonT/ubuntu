# Rclone Bidirectional Sync Filter Rules
#
# Rules are evaluated from top to bottom. The first match wins.
# '+' includes a directory or file, '-' excludes it.
# '/' anchors the match to the root of the synced folder.
# '**' matches everything recursively under that directory.

# --- Directories to Synchronize ---
+ /Desktop/**
+ /Documents/**
+ /Music/**
+ /Public/**
+ /Templates/**

# --- Exclude everything else ---
- **
